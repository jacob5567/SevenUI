## 0.1.0

- First release.
- HUD text uses the Barlow font.
- The run timer uses Barlow Condensed.
- The run info is one flat translucent panel, with a thin bar and the difficulty's name in place of the scrolling difficulty segments.
- The objective, artifact and LookingGlass stats panels are flat translucent panels, and the objective panel has a header strip.
