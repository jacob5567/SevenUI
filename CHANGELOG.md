## 0.1.0

- First release.
- HUD text uses the Barlow font.
- The HUD faces the screen squarely instead of its corners leaning inward.
- The run timer, the level, and skill and equipment cooldowns use Barlow Condensed.
- The health and experience bars are flat, and the health numbers are text instead of sprite digits.
- Money and the boss bar are flat, and the item bar and money lose their frames.
- Items sit on dark slots with their tier's color along the bottom, and stack counts are text.
- The skill and equipment slots are flat, with a top-down cooldown cover and small grey key labels.
- The run info is one flat translucent panel, with a thin bar and the difficulty's name in place of the scrolling difficulty segments.
- The objective, artifact and LookingGlass stats panels are flat translucent panels, and the objective panel has a header strip.
